About the Company

A Steadily Built Reputation for Construction Excellence

AGRAJ Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is a well-established construction company engaged in a wide range of infrastructure development projects. The company specializes in Road Construction Works, Building Construction (Residential, Commercial, Institutional), and Structural Works (Industrial Sheds, Bridges, etc.).

The company is led by Mr. Raj Kumar Singh Chauhan, a seasoned Civil Engineer with over 30 years of experience in the field. He initially established a proprietorship firm, M/s RAJ ASSOCIATES, in 1997, and later expanded his vision by incorporating AGRAJ Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. in 2012.

He is supported by his two sons—both qualified civil engineers—who play an active role in the management and execution of various projects. Their combined technical expertise and strong on-ground experience contribute to the company's continued growth and success.
