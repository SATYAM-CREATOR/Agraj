About the Company

Trusted Partner in Infrastructure Development

AGRAJ Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. is a well-established construction company engaged in a wide range of infrastructure development projects. With a commitment to quality, safety, and timely project delivery, AGRAJ Infrastructure has steadily built a reputation for excellence in the construction industry.

Led by Mr. Raj Kumar Singh Chauhan—a seasoned Civil Engineer with over 30 years of experience who founded M/s RAJ ASSOCIATES in 1997 and incorporated AGRAJ Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. in 2012—the company is actively driven by his two sons, both qualified civil engineers, combining deep technical expertise with strong on-ground execution.
